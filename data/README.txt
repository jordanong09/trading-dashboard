Put your IBKR Activity Statement CSV files in this folder.

  1. IBKR -> Performance & Reports -> Statements -> Activity
  2. Period: Year to Date   Format: CSV
  3. Download and drop the .csv file here (or upload it in the app sidebar).

The app loads the most recently modified CSV automatically.

NOTE: statement files contain your account number and trade history.
They are excluded from version control (.gitignore) and from the shared
package on purpose. Do not commit or share them.
